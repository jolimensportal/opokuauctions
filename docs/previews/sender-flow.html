Why $25.00 for a 20 kg box? Your box bills at 60 × 40 × 40 cm, which is 48 kg of air freight — more than the 20 kg on the scale. Charged on whichever is greater, and the container minimum of $25 applies. A 2 kg headlamp in the same box is also $25.00; a full pallet is $180.00.
