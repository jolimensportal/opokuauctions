Two things you flagged, with real numbers attached

The brown-red you disliked is text-brick-700 on bg-brick-50. It is not a contrast failure — it measures 13.98:1, comfortably AA. What is wrong with it is the hue: at oklch(0.405 0.155 27) it reads as a muddy brick brown rather than a confident colour, and it is used for headings across six files.

You asked for black on red. Measured honestly: black on the brand red (brick-500) is 2.66:1 and fails outright. Black on the red tint — which is what the card actually is — is 17.02:1. So the version of your instinct that works is the first one below.
