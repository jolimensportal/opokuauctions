What the seven demos actually agree on

Live demos opened and walked, not just read: Delix, CargoPro, We Courier, Movement, Deprixa.

Every one of them is a separate application

A side bar, a top bar and a breadcrumb. The public site and the account area never share chrome. We Courier ships four panels (admin, branch, merchant, plus two mobile apps); Delix has five (admin, account manager, branch manager, merchant, merchant staff).

The stat tile is the unit of a logistics dashboard

Circular icon, label, figure. We Courier: 8 tiles. CargoPro: 5 pipeline counters above one list. Delix: tiles with sparklines and delta chips. The reference screenshot: 3 tiles.

Lists are filter panels over dense rows

Search tracking · search customer · status · type, then a result count. The row carries identity, the customer, the money, a status pill and row actions — all on one line.

History is an event timeline, not a status field

Delix's parcel page: a green node on a rail, the event, who did it, and the timestamp. CargoPro adds "manual event entry" to the same timeline. Our milestones table already holds this, but no screen renders it yet.

Nav is grouped by consignment type

CargoPro: Prealerts, Shipments, Pickup, Consolidations — each with its own list, dashboard and payments, plus a shared Tools & Settings. Ours maps to two lanes, so two groups.

Money gets its own surface

Income and payout statement panels sit beside the counters, with COD, VAT, commission and merchant payable broken out. Freight billed is never a single number.
